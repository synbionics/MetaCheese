    What happened

    Raw reads are cleaned (P.1) and stripped of host DNA (P.2). The microbial reads then follow two branches: community-level profiling (C.1 to C.4) describes the whole community, assembly-specific profiling (A.1 to A.7) reconstructs genomes (MAGs). The last section (R.1) joins the two branches.
